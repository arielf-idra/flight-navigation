Settings

Keep your eyes on the attitude indicator. Every few seconds one other instrument appears for a brief moment and is then covered again. Catch it with a quick glance, return to the attitude indicator, then report what it showed.

Hold the attitude level while you scan: arrow keys (or W A S D) fly the aircraft, or drag on the attitude indicator. Up/W pushes the nose down, Down/S pulls it up. Answer with the number keys so your eyes can stay on the attitude indicator.

On a phone with tilt to fly on, the attitude indicator follows the phone: turn the phone like a wheel to bank, tilt the top toward you to raise the nose. Turbulence pushes the aircraft off, so tilt the phone to keep the miniature aircraft level on the horizon. The way you hold the phone at Start is level; Center resets it.

Instruments
A training aid for scan discipline only. Instrument markings and panel layout follow the Cessna 172S POH; the scan techniques follow the FAA Instrument Flying Handbook (FAA-H-8083-15B, ch. 6).
